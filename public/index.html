Peer-to-Peer Campus Tool & Equipment Library

Students and staff can list unused tools, electronics, lab kits and textbooks so other people on campus can borrow them instead of buying new ones.

Browse items Create account

How it works

1

Register

Make an account with your student/staff ID. Only campus users can use this site.

2

List or search

Add an item you don't use much, or search for something you need for a lab or assignment.

3

Borrow and return

Send a request with dates. The owner accepts or rejects it. After use, the owner marks it as returned.

Why this project

Many students buy cameras, kits and books for one semester and then they stay unused. Sharing these items can save money and also reduce electronic waste.
